ION Singapore’s first forum in Singapore

“TUNA World & Brain Economy Readiness: Why Neuroinclusion is Critical to the Future of Work,”

was more than an event. It was a powerful coming together of purpose, perspectives, and possibility.

It was heartening to see participants from within the ION ecosystem alongside many new voices joining us. 

We were privileged to hear from an exceptional panel of thought leaders, Professor Charlotte Valeur, Hazleen Ahmad, and Alister Ong, expertly guided by our moderator Subha Imtiaz. Their insights set the tone for how organisations must evolve in a rapidly changing world.

Several powerful themes emerged:
🔹 Rise of the Brain Economy - where cognitive capital is becoming a defining driver of competitiveness in a TUNA world: Turbulent, Uncertain, Novel, Ambiguous.
🔹 Neurodivergent talent as a strategic advantage - bringing strengths in innovation, complex problem-solving, pattern recognition, and creativity, capabilities increasingly critical in AI-augmented environments.
🔹 A shift from accommodation to intentional design - moving beyond retrofitting systems to designing workplaces where diverse cognitive strengths can thrive by default.
🔹 Leadership responsibility in shaping future ready organisations - recognising that success will depend on how effectively organisations harness diversity of thought.
🔹 Boardroom as the starting point for culture - sustainable neuroinclusion must begin at the governance level. Boards set the tone by embedding inclusion into strategy, leadership expectations, and organisational culture..

A memorable moment came during the Q&A, when participants stepped forward to share reflections and lived experiences.

Thank you to Charlotte Wilkinson, Nitin Goil, Jennifer Costigan, Atiqah Azhari, and Bhavana Chauhan for your openness and courage.

Being in that room with like-minded people created a powerful sense of collective energy and renewed belief that neurodivergent individuals are central to the future of work.

This forum marked an important milestone for ION Singapore, but it is only the beginning.
