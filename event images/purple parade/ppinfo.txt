We are proud to be part of The Purple Parade 2025. 

It’s more than a march. 
It’s a movement. One that celebrates all abilities and reminds us that inclusion is not a privilege, but a right. 

At ION Singapore, we believe in a society where every neurominority is understood, supported and empowered to thrive. 

We work to build awareness, representation and real opportunities in schools, workplaces and communities. 

A heartfelt thank you to Gayathri Ramaswami and her amazing inclusive school, All Hands Together, for organising and leading our group at the march. Your dedication to inclusion inspires us all. 

We also honour Professor Charlotte Valeur, Founder of Institute Of Neurodiversity (ION), whose vision for a neuro-inclusive world continues to guide our mission. 

And we’re deeply grateful to Hazleen Ahmad, Founder and Chair of ION Singapore and Deputy CEO of ION Global, and to A Rohim Sarip BBM,PBM, President of ION Singapore, for their leadership and commitment to building a stronger, more inclusive community.

Standing alongside thousands at The Purple Parade, we celebrated what truly makes Singapore strong: our diversity. 

Let’s keep the conversation going. 

If you believe in a neuro-inclusive Singapore, we’d love to connect!
